The game has a player character near the bottom of the screen that moves left and right by tapping either side of the screen. The player must not be able to move past the screen edges, and movement should feel smooth rather than snapping instantly. Player movement speed should fall somewhere in the range of 150 to 300 pixels per second.

Two types of objects fall from the top of the screen throughout the game. One type is a hazard the player must avoid, and the other is a collectible the player wants to touch. The two types must look different from each other so the player can tell them apart at a glance. Collectibles should fall at a speed in the range of 80 to 150 pixels per second. Hazards should fall noticeably faster than collectibles, at a speed in the range of 180 to 300 pixels per second. Both types must spawn at varied horizontal positions and keep appearing throughout the session on their own independent schedules, so the player is always reacting to something new. A reasonable target is a new collectible every 2 to 4 seconds and a new hazard every 1 to 3 seconds, though exact values within those ranges are left to the implementer.
